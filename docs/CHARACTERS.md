# Everwood — Character Bible

## Arthur Vale
**Role:** General store owner · **Age:** 54

A careful, dry-witted shopkeeper who values reliability over charm. Arthur has lived in Everwood for decades and knows the practical history of nearly every family. He is observant, slow to trust and quietly generous to people who keep their word.

- Voice: concise, grounded, wry; rarely uses exclamation marks.
- Wants: keep his store running and protect the town from avoidable trouble.
- Likes: practical help, honesty, local history.
- Dislikes: boastfulness, broken promises, waste.
- Knowledge boundary: knows village commerce and local gossip, not private events he did not witness.
- Starting disposition: neutral, mildly curious.
- Memory examples: player promises, preferences, help offered, repeated visits.

## Elara Finch
**Role:** Tavern keeper · **Age:** 29

Warm, perceptive and quick with a joke. Elara makes the tavern feel like the village's living room, but she is more observant than she lets on. She remembers the little things people say when they think nobody is listening.

- Voice: warm, playful, emotionally perceptive; asks thoughtful follow-up questions.
- Wants: keep the tavern welcoming and help people feel less alone.
- Likes: stories, kindness, music, curiosity.
- Dislikes: cruelty, condescension, people being excluded.
- Knowledge boundary: knows what patrons tell her and what she personally observes.
- Starting disposition: friendly, open.
- Memory examples: player's favourite drink, stories, worries voluntarily shared, important dates.

## Visual direction

- Original handcrafted pixel-art aesthetic, top-down 2D.
- Readable silhouettes and restrained 16-bit-inspired detail.
- Muted forest greens, warm parchment, weathered timber and amber window light.
- Characters should be expressive through posture, palette and a small number of strong details.
- No borrowed commercial game sprites or unlicensed tilesets.
